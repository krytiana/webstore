Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Welcome to Code CartHub. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website, products, services, account features, downloads, and AI consultant.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is protected, and the choices available to you.

1. About Code CartHub

Code CartHub operates from Ghana and provides website templates, source code, website setup services, downloads, technical support, and an AI-powered consultant.

Code CartHub is intended primarily for adults and business users. Users must be at least 13 years old to use the service.

2. Information We Collect

When you create an account, purchase a product, request support, download a product, or use certain features of our website, we may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password
  • Phone number
  • Country

We may also collect information related to your purchases, downloads, account activity, and interactions with our services.

3. Payment Information

Payments on Code CartHub are processed through Paystack.

Payment processing is handled by Paystack. Code CartHub does not directly store your complete payment card details.

Paystack may collect and process payment information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use information collected through Code CartHub for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts
  • Providing purchased products and downloads
  • Processing and confirming purchases
  • Providing customer and technical support
  • Sending account-related emails
  • Sending purchase confirmations
  • Sending download links
  • Sending verification emails
  • Sending promotional and marketing communications
  • Monitoring download activity
  • Preventing abuse of download systems
  • Improving our website and services
  • Understanding website traffic and usage

5. Downloads and Purchase Records

To protect digital products and prevent unauthorized sharing, Code CartHub records download activity.

Download links may expire after 48 hours.

Download links may also be limited to a maximum of 3 successful downloads.

Purchase and download records may be retained indefinitely unless you request deletion of your account and applicable data.

6. AI Consultant

Code CartHub provides an AI-powered consultant to help users with questions about our products, services, website templates, and technical matters.

The AI consultant is powered by an external AI service provider, Groq.

Information provided to the AI consultant

When you use the AI consultant and provide consent through the applicable consent flow, the consultant may receive certain information associated with your account, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Country
AI conversation privacy: We do not store every AI conversation. Only conversations or information identified as leads may be retained for team review when the user has provided the required consent.

Lead conversations may be retained indefinitely to help us review customer inquiries and improve the service experience.

Authorized Code CartHub administrators may review lead conversations where the user has provided the required consent.

7. Email Communications

We may send emails relating to your account and use of our services, including:

  • Account registration
  • Email verification
  • Purchase confirmations
  • Download links
  • Important service notifications
  • Customer support communications

We may also send promotional and marketing emails about Code CartHub, new products, updates, and related services.

Marketing emails include an unsubscribe option. You can unsubscribe from promotional communications at any time.

8. Analytics

Code CartHub uses Google Analytics to understand website traffic and usage.

Analytics may help us understand information such as how visitors interact with the website, which pages are viewed, and general website usage patterns.

We do not currently use Google AdSense, Google Search Console, Facebook Pixel, or TikTok Pixel for advertising or tracking purposes.

9. Cookies and Session Technologies

Code CartHub uses cookies and session-related technologies to help operate the website and maintain user sessions.

These technologies may be used to keep you signed in, maintain account functionality, support website features, and understand website usage.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Security measures may include encryption and appropriate technical and organizational safeguards.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate Code CartHub and provide our services.

These may include providers used for:

  • Payment processing
  • AI services
  • Email delivery
  • Website hosting
  • Database services
  • Analytics
  • File and image services

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

12. Your Privacy Choices

You may request that we correct inaccurate personal information associated with your account.

You may also request deletion of your account and applicable personal information.

We do not currently provide a dedicated automated tool for requesting a copy of all personal information we hold. You may contact us if you have questions about the information associated with your account.

If you wish to delete your account, you may submit a request through your account dashboard where available or contact us using one of the privacy email addresses listed below.

Some information may need to be retained where required for legal, security, fraud prevention, accounting, or legitimate business purposes.

Code CartHub is intended for adults and business users and is not directed toward children under 13.
